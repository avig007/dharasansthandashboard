Add a month, a week or a whole year at once. No fixed format is needed: the dashboard reads the file, matches the columns and shows a preview before anything is saved.

Files it accepts

✔ Dhara Sansthan’s monthly “Statement of Receipts & Payments”: upload it as it is. Each month sheet is read, and the month is taken from the sheet title.

✔ Any sheet with one row per ATM: column names are matched automatically (e.g. “Name of the Plant”, “No. of Cans”, “Revenu Total”, “Operator Salary”). You can correct the matching, or ask AI to do it.

✔ The simple template (below), if you prefer: ATM · Period · Cans · Revenue · Operator pay · Electricity · Repairs and consumables · Other costs.

Must have: ATM name, cans sold, revenue, and the month (a column, the sheet title or the sheet name). Costs are optional. Weekly rows are added up into months. Re-uploading the same ATM and period replaces the old figures.
